about

Abhishek Shukla.

I started building mobile apps in January 2013 and spent the next decade on mobile and backend systems at product companies in Bangalore. Today I'm an engineering manager.

Over the last year I moved into AI systems, and I learn in public. Before a part is scripted, I run the experiment behind it myself, so every "I" in a reel is something I actually did. The series is the record of that move; the workshops are the hands-on version of it, for engineers making the same one.

No hype. No tool lists.
